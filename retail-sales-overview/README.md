# 🏬 Retail Sales Overview

A clean one-page dashboard summarising retail sales for the first half of the year — receipts, sales, cost and profit by month, region, payment type and product.

![Retail Sales Overview](images/dashboard.png)

## 🎯 Business questions

- How are sales and profit developing **month by month**?
- Which **regions** and **products** sell the most?
- How do customers **pay**?

## 📈 Key metrics (January – June)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Receipts | **36** |
| Sales | **$46K** |
| Quantity | **227** |
| Cost | **$28K** |
| Profit | **$18K** |
| Profit % | **40%** |
| Avg sale | **$1.28K** |

## 💡 Insights

- **May** was the strongest month, with sales above $10K.
- **North America** leads in sales, followed by Asia and Europe; the Middle East is the smallest region.
- **Laptop Pro 14** dominates with **$31K** — roughly two-thirds of all sales — so results depend heavily on one product.
- **Customer account** is the main payment type (57%), followed by card (29%) and cash (14%).
- The business keeps a stable **40% profit margin** across months.

## ⚙️ Features

- KPI cards for the main sales metrics
- Monthly sales trend and sales vs cost vs profit comparison
- Breakdowns by region, payment type and product
